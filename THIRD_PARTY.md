## Platform and data

[FBInk](https://github.com/NiLuJe/FBInk), by NiLuJe and contributors, is GPL-3.0
software supplied by the jailbreak environment. It is not bundled here.
The US time-zone files are public-domain IANA data copied from the setup Mac's
installed time-zone database.

Weather: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), alerts:
[National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api),
ZIP lookup: [Zippopotam.us](https://www.zippopotam.us/).

The Paperwhite power-management approach was informed by
[Esteban Carisimo's paperwhite-weather](https://github.com/estcarisimo/paperwhite-weather).
Its MIT copyright notice is retained in `third_party/paperwhite-weather-LICENSE`.
Jailbreak and scriptlet documentation: [KindleModding](https://kindlemodding.org/).
